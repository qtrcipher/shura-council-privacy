Privacy Policy

Shura Council App — Last updated: March 27, 2026

Introduction

Shura Council ("the App") is a secure workplace messaging and collaboration platform developed for the Qatar Shura Council. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

Information We Collect

When you use the App, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, department, and organizational role, provided during account registration.
  • Messages and Content: Text messages, images, and files you send through the App, stored securely in our database.
  • Device Information: Device tokens for push notifications to deliver messages to your device.
  • Usage Data: Online status and last seen timestamps to support presence features within the App.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Provide secure messaging and collaboration services within the Shura Council.
  • Deliver push notifications for new messages, events, and announcements.
  • Display your profile to other authorized members of the organization.
  • Manage organizational roles, permissions, and committee assignments.
  • Facilitate event scheduling, delegation coordination, and news distribution.

Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest. Access to data is restricted to authenticated members of the organization through role-based access controls.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only accessible to:

  • Other authorized members of the Shura Council within the App.
  • System administrators for organizational management purposes.

We use Google Firebase services for authentication, data storage, file storage, and push notifications. Google's privacy policy applies to their infrastructure services.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the App.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information through your administrator.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active within the organization. Upon account deletion, your profile data is removed. Message history may be retained for organizational record-keeping purposes.

Push Notifications

The App uses push notifications to alert you of new messages, events, and announcements. You can manage notification preferences within the App settings or through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for authorized members of the Shura Council and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact us at dev.hamam@gmail.com.
